Rhymescape

Write bars. Watch every rhyme light up.

Rhymescape is printed like a risograph zine: warm stock, blue-black ink, and loud fluorescent passes that overprint wherever words rhyme. Every syllable sits on a 16-slot beat grid and every rhyme chain gets its own ink, marker and line style, so flow is something you can see as well as hear.

01 · Type

Two voices: one shouts, one counts.

Bricolage Grotesque is the loud, bendy headline voice. Martian Mono is the counting voice: wide, even cells that make every syllable land on its slot.

02 · Palette

Stock, ink, and five fluorescent passes.

Surfaces are warm paper or black card. Text is blue-black ink, never pure black. Fluorescent inks are decoration and fills; text on them is always blue-black ink. Everything is OKLCH, so lightness is honest across hues.

Stock + ink

  • Paper--paper
  • Raised sheet--paper-raised
  • Sunk tray--paper-sunk
  • Ink--ink
  • Soft ink--ink-soft

Fluorescent inks · the same on both stocks

  • Hot pink--fl-pink
  • Orange--fl-orange
  • Yellow--fl-yellow
  • Mint--fl-mint
  • Blue--fl-blue

Overprint: where two inks overlap they multiply into a third colour. Used for stickers and for the highlighter wash when two chains meet.

    03 · Rhyme chains

    Any number of chains. Never just a colour.

    Chains are generated, not listed. Hue steps by the golden angle so neighbours never clash; lightness is then solved per chain until it clears 4.5:1 on the hardest stock. Shape, line style and a letter carry the same information, so colour is never the only signal.

    8
    1. Hue (354° + n × 137.508°) mod 360. Golden angle: every new chain lands in the biggest gap.
    2. Value Step lightness away from the stock until contrast ≥ 4.7 against paper, raised and sunk. Chroma follows the sRGB gamut edge.
    3. Wash A highlighter fill, solved so body ink on it also clears 4.7:1.
    4. Signal 8 marker shapes × 5 line styles × a unique letter. Combinations stay unique for 40 chains, letters forever.

        Shape · double bezel

        inner = outer − bezel

        Every plate is a tray holding a sheet: 28px outer, 6px bezel, 22px inner, so the curves stay concentric. Offsets are hard, coloured "misregistration" shadows, never soft black blurs. Pills (999px) for buttons and chips.
